Privacy notice · Updated 1 November 2026

Privacy and external services

Lune_X is a static informational website without visitor accounts, newsletter, checkout, project-installed profiling or analytics.

Controller and contact

Project lead: Marcelo Javier Fernandez Caballero. Until a dedicated public email address is available, privacy enquiries can be sent through the official Instagram profile. Please do not send sensitive information in public messages.

Technical data and hosting

This website does not gather personal data through forms. Browsing necessarily transmits technical information including IP address, browser details, requests and timestamps to GitHub Pages, which hosts and secures the website. GitHub handles logs according to its privacy statement.

External fonts

Some pages request fonts via Google Fonts. This can transmit connection information to Google. Read Google's privacy policy.

Spotify players and other external services

Embedded Spotify players load only following an explicit click. Spotify may then process technical information or use cookies according to its own policies. Links to Apple Music, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and DistroKid open independent services with their own notices. Spotify privacy · Apple privacy.

Cookies and purposes

The project does not intentionally install first-party analytics, advertising or profiling cookies. Third-party services activated by a visitor may use cookies and similar technologies. Technical data processing supports availability and security; loading an external player is a voluntary choice.

Retention and international transfers

The project does not keep its own database of visitors. Technical log retention, third-party recipients and possible transfers outside the European Economic Area depend on each provider's published terms and policies.

Your rights

When GDPR applies, you may seek information, access, rectification, erasure, restriction or objection, and complain to the Italian Data Protection Authority. Contact individual providers directly regarding data they control independently.

This notice reflects the website's technical configuration on 1 November 2026. It must be reviewed if forms, analytics, newsletters, advertising or additional services are introduced.
