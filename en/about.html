The project and its vision

Lune_X is a virtual music identity guided by human creativity

An openly communicated creative process

The project was conceived and is creatively directed by Marcelo Javier Fernandez Caballero. Tracks are made with a paid Suno Pro account and distributed through DistroKid. Artistic decisions, themes, track selection and visual identity are part of its human creative direction

Lune_X is not presented as a real person or a live singer. The use of AI production tools is disclosed, while commercial rights depend on the relevant contractual conditions for each recording

Transparency and rights

Lune_X discloses the use of generative tools in music production and its human creative direction. Listening services are third parties with their own terms.
