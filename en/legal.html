Legal notices and music rights

Lune_X is an independent music project created and directed by Marcelo Javier Fernandez Caballero. It uses generative tools under human artistic supervision; Lune_X is not presented as a human performer.

Music production and commercial use

According to its creative director, Lune_X music is produced through a paid Suno Pro subscription and distributed via DistroKid. Under applicable Suno terms, qualifying outputs created and downloaded under the plan's conditions can be used commercially. Marcelo Javier Fernandez Caballero manages the contractual commercial usage and distribution rights available for the Lune_X catalogue.

Commercial rights versus copyright

Commercial usage rights from Suno do not automatically guarantee copyright protection for every AI-generated composition or recording, or freedom from third-party claims. Legal protection depends on applicable law, verifiable human creative contributions and underlying material. No blanket exclusive copyright claim is made over automatically generated output.

Project creator and human creative contribution

Marcelo Javier Fernandez Caballero created Lune_X and is responsible for its artistic and executive direction, including concepts, creative decisions, track selection, revisions, visuals and release approvals. Records of human creative choices may help establish whether specific works qualify for copyright protection. Under Italian law, AI-assisted works require a demonstrable human intellectual contribution; merely approving an AI output does not automatically establish exclusive copyright.

Recording-by-recording rights verification

The stated Suno Pro subscription is the contractual basis for commercial use of eligible music outputs. Under the terms effective from 3 September 2026, commercial exploitation requires, among other conditions, an authorised download and compliance with applicable restrictions, including those for remixes. Each release should have a traceable master, subscription status, permitted download and third-party rights check. This notice does not claim that the entire catalogue has already undergone individual rights verification.

AI-generated artwork, avatars and voices

Cover artwork, promotional imagery, avatars and videos may be created using services other than Suno. Their use depends on each service's applicable licence and is not automatically covered by a Suno Pro subscription. Lune_X aims to avoid unauthorised imitation of identifiable real people, voices and identities, and to check permissions for third-party materials.

AI transparency in audiovisual content

Lune_X discloses the use of artificial intelligence in music and visual production. Where relevant, disclosures to audiences and platforms are assessed based on the content and applicable requirements, including Article 50 of the EU AI Act, applicable from 2 August 2026. Not every AI-generated illustration is automatically a deepfake; artistic and fictional content may follow context-appropriate disclosure provisions.

Reuse and permission requests

Streaming, viewing promotional previews and sharing official links do not grant third parties a licence for synchronisation, sampling, redistribution or commercial use. Requests are assessed individually against the rights actually available. Editorial and licensing enquiries should use the project's official channels.

DistroKid distribution

DistroKid permits the distribution of AI-generated music where the uploader holds the required rights and follows platform policies, including restrictions on impersonation and infringement. The distributor does not automatically take ownership of uploaded recordings.

Site content and music previews

Short MP3 excerpts hosted on this site are promotional previews from the Lune_X catalogue. Their availability does not grant permission to republish, sell, redistribute or synchronize the recordings. Rights in protectable human-created artwork, text, branding and other content are reserved to the extent permitted by law.

Featured human artist

The feature on Pier Paolo Piccioni is an independent editorial recommendation of a Spotify release; it does not imply a collaboration, endorsement or sponsorship. The recording, artwork and trademarks remain subject to their respective rights holders.

Apple Music and royalties

Apple Music receives editorial prominence by the project's commercial choice. The site makes no unverified promise of higher payouts or guaranteed income. Royalty amounts depend on contracts, territories, subscriptions and platform statements.
