Legal notices and music rights

Lune_X is a virtual musical identity under the creative direction of Marcelo Javier Fernandez Caballero. The project is not presented as a human performer.

Music production and commercial use

According to its creative director, Lune_X music is produced through a paid Suno Pro subscription and distributed via DistroKid. Under applicable Suno terms, qualifying outputs created and downloaded under the plan's conditions can be used commercially. Marcelo Javier Fernandez Caballero manages the contractual commercial usage and distribution rights available for the Lune_X catalogue.

Commercial rights versus copyright

Commercial usage rights from Suno do not automatically guarantee copyright protection for every AI-generated composition or recording, or freedom from third-party claims. Legal protection depends on applicable law, verifiable human creative contributions and underlying material. No blanket exclusive copyright claim is made over automatically generated output.

DistroKid distribution

DistroKid permits the distribution of AI-generated music where the uploader holds the required rights and follows platform policies, including restrictions on impersonation and infringement. The distributor does not automatically take ownership of uploaded recordings.
