Behind the frequency · Our story

Not a face to imitate
A story worth listening to

Lune_X begins with a human decision: to build musical worlds shaped by desire, movement and belonging. Generative tools are part of the production process, but creative choices define the project's direction. We don't ask you to believe in a character: we invite you to listen to the music.

The night can be a turning point, a refuge or the beginning of a journey. Every listener brings their own story.

Our creative process

Lune_X is a virtual artistic identity, not a real person. Human creative direction shapes the atmosphere, aesthetics and vision; tracks are produced with a paid Suno Pro subscription and distributed through DistroKid.

We are transparent about the technology; our connection with listeners is built through music. The project does not claim live performances or attribute human emotions to software.
