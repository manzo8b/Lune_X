Rights and transparency come first

Lune_X is a virtual music identity with human creative direction and AI-assisted production. Sending an enquiry grants no licence. Any economic agreement, including rights, exclusivity and usage scope, requires a specific review and contract. The human guest artist's work is not part of the Lune_X catalogue offered for collaboration
